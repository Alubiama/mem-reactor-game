ONE CAKE. ONE SCREEN. YOUR CHAOS.

On your turn, add a useful mutation that causes trouble, or reinvent the latest trouble to fix it. Pass the screen after each move.

No sign-in, wallet, points or prizes. A round takes a few minutes. Solo mode lets you test every turn yourself.

Each choice changes the courier. An unfixed problem can come back in the ending.
